# Time decay and theta

## What theta means
Theta measures how much an option's price falls per day from the passage of time, all else equal. A theta of -0.40 means the option loses about $0.40 per share per day, or about $40 per standard contract of 100 shares.

## Decay speeds up near expiration
Time decay is not linear. An at-the-money option loses value slowly when expiration is months away and much faster in the final weeks and days. In the last few days, most of the remaining time value can disappear, which makes very short-dated long options a race against the clock.

## Who benefits from decay
Option buyers pay theta; option sellers collect it. A buyer needs the stock to move far enough and soon enough to outrun decay. Choosing a later expiration reduces daily decay but costs more up front.
