# Delta

## Sensitivity to the stock price
Delta estimates how much an option's price changes for a $1 move in the stock. A call with a delta of 0.30 gains about $0.30 per share if the stock rises $1. Calls have deltas between 0 and 1; puts have deltas between -1 and 0.

## A rough probability gauge
Delta is often used as a rough estimate of the chance the option finishes in the money. A 0.30-delta call is very roughly a 30% chance under the model's assumptions. It is an approximation, not a forecast, and it changes as the stock moves and time passes.
