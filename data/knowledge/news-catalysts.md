# News and catalysts

## Scheduled versus unscheduled events
Scheduled catalysts include earnings reports, product launches, investor days and economic data releases; their dates are known and option prices usually reflect them. Unscheduled news, such as lawsuits or management changes, can move a stock without warning.

## Using news carefully
Headlines show what the market is already discussing, so most widely reported news is already priced in. Duplicate coverage of one story across many outlets does not make it more important. Older news is less likely to move the stock than a fresh, unexpected development.
