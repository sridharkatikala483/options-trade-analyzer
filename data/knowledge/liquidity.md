# Liquidity: bid-ask spread, volume and open interest

## The bid-ask spread is a hidden cost
The bid is what buyers will pay and the ask is what sellers want. Buying at the ask and later selling at the bid loses the spread. A spread of $0.10 on a $0.60 option is about 17% of the price, a large cost before the stock even moves. Wide spreads usually signal an illiquid contract.

## Volume and open interest
Volume is the number of contracts traded today; open interest is the number of contracts currently outstanding. Low volume and low open interest mean fewer participants, wider spreads and difficulty exiting at a fair price. As a rough guide, contracts with tight spreads and open interest in the thousands are easier to trade than ones with only a few dozen.
