# Position sizing and risk

## Size by the maximum loss
Because a long option can lose its entire premium, a common guideline is to size each trade so that losing the full premium is acceptable, often a small percentage of the account. Option leverage makes it easy to take far more risk than intended.

## Probability versus payoff
Cheap out-of-the-money options offer large percentage payoffs with low probabilities. Expensive in-the-money options have higher probabilities with smaller percentage payoffs. Neither is better on its own; the trade-off should match the trader's view and risk tolerance.
