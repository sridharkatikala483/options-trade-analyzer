# Breakeven and maximum loss for long options

## Breakeven at expiration
For a long call, the breakeven stock price at expiration is the strike plus the premium paid. For a long put, it is the strike minus the premium. Below the call breakeven (or above the put breakeven) the trade loses money at expiration, even if the option finishes in the money.

## Maximum loss
The most a buyer of a call or put can lose is the premium paid, times 100 shares per contract. This loss happens if the option expires out of the money. Limited loss does not mean low risk: losing the full premium is a common outcome for short-dated, out-of-the-money options.
