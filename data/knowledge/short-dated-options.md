# Very short-dated options

## Expiring within days
Options that expire within a few days have little time value left, extreme time decay and very high gamma. Their outcome depends almost entirely on one or two days of stock movement.

## Risks for buyers
For a buyer, a short-dated out-of-the-money option behaves like a lottery ticket: a small chance of a large percentage gain and a high chance of losing the whole premium. Being right about the direction is not enough if the move does not happen before expiration.
