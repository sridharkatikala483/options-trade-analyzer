# Dividends and early assignment

## Dividends lower call values
When a stock goes ex-dividend, its price typically drops by about the dividend amount, which reduces call values and raises put values. Expected dividends before expiration are already reflected in option prices.

## Early assignment risk
Holders of in-the-money calls sometimes exercise early to capture a dividend. Sellers of those calls can be assigned unexpectedly the day before the ex-dividend date. Buyers of options do not face assignment, only sellers.
