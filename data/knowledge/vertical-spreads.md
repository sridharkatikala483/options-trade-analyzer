# Vertical spreads

## Structure
A bull call spread buys one call and sells another call with a higher strike and the same expiration. The sold call lowers the cost, and the maximum gain is capped at the difference between the strikes minus the net premium paid.

## When spreads help
Because one option is bought and one sold, a spread is less exposed to implied volatility and time decay than a single long option. This makes spreads a common defined-risk alternative when implied volatility is high, such as before earnings. The maximum loss is the net premium paid.
