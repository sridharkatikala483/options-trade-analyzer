# Moneyness: in, at and out of the money

## Definitions
A call is in the money when the stock is above the strike, at the money when they are about equal, and out of the money when the stock is below the strike. For puts it is the reverse.

## What moneyness means for a buyer
Out-of-the-money options are cheap because they consist only of time value and need a move just to reach the strike. In-the-money options cost more but behave more like the stock itself. At-the-money options carry the most time value and therefore the most time decay in dollar terms.
