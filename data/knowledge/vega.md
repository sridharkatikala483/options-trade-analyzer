# Vega

## Sensitivity to implied volatility
Vega estimates how much an option's price changes when implied volatility moves by one percentage point. A vega of 0.15 means the option gains about $0.15 per share if IV rises from 30% to 31%, and loses the same if IV falls.

## Why vega matters around events
Longer-dated options have more vega than short-dated ones. Before events such as earnings, a long option's value depends heavily on IV, so a post-event drop in IV can erase gains from the stock's move. Multiplying vega by the expected IV drop gives a quick estimate of the loss from volatility crush.
