# Cash-secured puts

## Structure
A cash-secured put sells a put while holding enough cash to buy 100 shares at the strike if assigned. The seller collects premium and agrees to buy the stock at the strike.

## Risk profile
If the stock stays above the strike, the seller keeps the premium. If it falls below, the seller buys shares at the strike, cushioned only by the premium received. It suits someone willing to own the stock at that price.
