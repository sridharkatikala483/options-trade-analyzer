# Gamma

## How fast delta changes
Gamma measures how much delta changes for a $1 move in the stock. High gamma means the option's sensitivity can swing quickly, so its value becomes very jumpy.

## Gamma near expiration
Gamma is highest for at-the-money options close to expiration. In the final days a small stock move can turn an option from nearly worthless to valuable, or the reverse. This makes short-dated at-the-money options behave like all-or-nothing bets.
